# 🎉 Lava is the Floor 🎉

> **A game made with ❤️ by Fletchi for Henrik's birthday!** 🎂

This document contains all acknowledgements, licenses, and attributions for all tools, music tracks, sound effects, and 3D assets used in the project according to their respective licensing terms.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Game Engine:** [Godot Engine 4.x](https://godotengine.org/) (MIT License) – Huge thanks to Juan Linietsky, Ariel Manzur, and the global Godot community!
- **3D Modeling & Adjustments:** [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) (GNU GPL) – Mesh creation, adjustments, and animations.
- **Multiplayer Networking:** [Netfox](https://github.com/foxssake/netfox) & [Noray Relay](https://github.com/foxssake/noray) by Foxssake – High-level multiplayer state synchronization and NAT traversal relay server.
- **Level Design Tool:** [Go Placer](https://github.com/bramreth/go-placer) by bramreth – In-editor 3D asset placement tool for Godot.
- **Shaders & FX:** Custom Molten Lava Topdown Shader (vibecoded with AI assistance).
- **Custom UI & Fonts:** Dynamic Control nodes, RichTextLabel styling, and procedural sky environments.

### 2. Kenney Game Assets (CC0 1.0 Universal)

Created by **Kenney** ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl)).  
All models are dedicated to the public domain under the [Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) license.

#### A. Furniture Kit (v2.0)
- **Model Count:** 140 `.glb` meshes
- **Location:** `assets/floor_is_lava/furniture/`
- **Source:** [Kenney Furniture Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit)
- **Use Cases:** Living room, bedroom, and kitchen parkour levels ("The Floor is Lava at Home").
- **Included Objects:**
  - Sofas (2-seater, 3-seater, corner sofas, armchairs, pillows)
  - Coffee tables, dining tables, side tables, desks
  - Beds: Bunk bed (`bedBunk.glb`), single beds, double beds
  - Bookshelves (open, closed, wide, low) and stacks of books
  - Kitchen units, cabinets, refrigerator, oven/stove, sink, toaster, microwave
  - Bathroom objects: Bathtub, sink, toilet, mirror
  - Lighting: Floor lamps, ceiling lamps, desk lamps
  - Electronics: CRT TVs, flat TVs, speakers, radios, monitors
  - Decoration: Houseplants, clocks, trash cans

#### B. Retro Urban Kit
- **Model Count:** 124 `.glb` meshes
- **Location:** `assets/floor_is_lava/urban_fire_escape/`
- **Source:** [Kenney Retro Urban Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/retro-urban-kit)
- **Use Cases:** Building facades, alleyways, and urban rooftops.
- **Included Objects:**
  - Fire escapes: Platforms, balconies, stairs, ladders, railings
  - Dumpsters, street trash bins
  - Rooftop AC units, ventilation ducts, pipes
  - Scaffolding, barriers, wooden fences, awnings

#### C. Factory Kit (v3.0)
- **Model Count:** 143 `.glb` meshes
- **Location:** `assets/floor_is_lava/industrial_warehouse/`
- **Source:** [Kenney Factory Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/factory-kit)
- **Use Cases:** Warehouse, factory, and basement levels with pipe runs and stacked crates.
- **Included Objects:**
  - Stackable crates and boxes (`box-small`, `box-large`, `box-long`, `box-wide`)
  - Detailed piping systems: Straights, bends, T-crosses, 4-way crosses, valves
  - Industrial catwalks, stairs, safety railings
  - Oil drums, industrial tanks, warning signs, floor buttons

#### D. Mini Skate Kit
